# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 贡献者行为准则

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

我们承诺：每位参与者（无论年龄、体型、残障状况、种族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向）都能在本社区中获得不受骚扰的体验。

## Our Standards / 行为标准

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language / 使用友好包容的语言
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints / 尊重不同观点
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism / 坦然接受建设性批评
- Focusing on what is best for the community / 以社区利益为重

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks / 骚扰、挑衅或人身攻击
- Publishing others' private information / 公开他人隐私信息
- Any form of discrimination / 任何形式的歧视

## Enforcement / 执行

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project team via GitHub
issue (non-sensitive) or private security contact. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated.

违反行为准则的报告可通过 GitHub Issue（非敏感事项）或私下渠道提交，所有投诉都将得到审查与处理。
